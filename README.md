# Marketing Intelligence Dashboard
### Executive Analytics Report

> Transforming marketing data into executive insights through interactive Business Intelligence.

---

# Executive Summary

Modern marketing campaigns generate millions of customer interactions across multiple advertising channels. While raw metrics are valuable, business leaders require a concise and actionable view of campaign performance to support strategic decision-making.

This project presents an executive-level Power BI dashboard designed to monitor marketing efficiency, evaluate advertising performance, and identify optimization opportunities through interactive visual analytics.

Rather than displaying isolated metrics, the dashboard tells the complete story of marketing performance—from investment and engagement to conversion outcomes.

---

# Business Context

Marketing teams often manage campaigns across several acquisition channels, advertising platforms, and audience segments.

Without centralized reporting, answering critical business questions becomes difficult:

- Are we investing efficiently?
- Which channels generate the highest engagement?
- Is campaign performance improving?
- Are acquisition costs increasing?
- Which campaigns deserve additional investment?
- Where should marketing budgets be reduced?

This dashboard consolidates those answers into a single executive reporting solution.

# KPI Definitions

| KPI | Business Meaning |
|------|------------------|
| Spend | Total advertising investment |
| CPM | Cost per thousand impressions |
| CPC | Average cost per click |
| CTR | Percentage of users clicking advertisements |
| Impressions | Total advertisement views |
| Video Views | Number of video interactions |
| Conversions | Desired customer actions completed |
| Conversion Rate | Percentage of visitors converted |

# Executive Insights

## Marketing Investment Increased

Total advertising spend reached **98.58K**, representing additional investment compared to the previous reporting period.

Although overall investment increased, efficiency metrics remained stable, indicating controlled budget expansion.

**Business Impact**

The organization successfully scaled advertising investment without significant performance deterioration.

---

## Advertising Efficiency Improved

CPM decreased despite higher marketing spend.

This suggests that impressions were purchased more efficiently, allowing broader campaign exposure without proportional cost increases.

**Recommendation**

Continue negotiating media buying strategies while maintaining audience quality.

---

## Engagement Slightly Declined

CTR experienced a small decrease.

While performance remains healthy, the trend could indicate:

- Creative fatigue
- Audience saturation
- Reduced ad relevance

**Recommendation**

Launch creative A/B testing and refresh campaign assets.

---

## Lower Traffic Acquisition Cost

CPC declined slightly compared to the previous period.

Lower acquisition costs improve campaign profitability and provide opportunities to increase traffic volume without increasing overall investment.

---

## Strong Brand Visibility

The campaigns generated:

- 211K Impressions
- 122K Video Views

These figures indicate strong market exposure and successful awareness generation.

---

## Healthy Conversion Performance

The dashboard reports a conversion rate close to **10%**, suggesting that traffic quality remains strong even as engagement fluctuates.

This indicates that landing pages and conversion funnels continue converting visitors effectively.

---

## Channel Performance

Programmatic advertising consistently generated the highest traffic volume across most months.

Paid Search demonstrated recovery during the final months of the reporting period, while Organic traffic remained relatively stable.

This diversification reduces dependency on a single acquisition source.

---

## Seasonality

Monthly trends reveal a noticeable performance peak around mid-year followed by gradual normalization.

Possible drivers include:

- Seasonal demand
- Budget distribution
- Campaign lifecycle
- Audience behavior

Recognizing these patterns enables more effective future campaign planning.
